You’re right: there’s no separate “Gantt Chart” menu option. 
The chart is shown automatically when you run a scheduling policy.

From the main menu, choose [02] Temporal Simulation, then choose 
FCFS, Round Robin, or Priority Scheduling. The timeline appears 
during the run, and the final chart appears in the execution 
report. You can also choose Compare policies to see each policy’s 
timeline.
That behavior is implemented in [ui/Menu.h (line 311)](/F:/Projects/Dsa_miniPro/ui/Menu.h:311). run.txt only contains the
 build and launch commands, so it won’t show a menu option.
